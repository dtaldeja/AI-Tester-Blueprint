AI (Artificial Intelligence) — the
  big umbrella. Any machine that
  mimics human intelligence. Like a
  robot that can do tasks humans do.
  Example: Alexa understanding your
  voice and playing music.

  ML (Machine Learning) — a subset of
   AI. The machine learns from data
  (examples) instead of being
  explicitly programmed with rules.
  Example: Your email spam filter —
  it learns which emails are spam by
  seeing thousands of examples you
  mark as spam.

  DL (Deep Learning) — a subset of
  ML. Uses brain-inspired neural
  networks with many layers, learning
   patterns from massive amounts of
  data.
  Example: Google Photos recognizing
  your dog in a photo, or ChatGPT
  understanding and writing text.

  The onion analogy: AI is the whole
  onion, ML is one layer inside it,
  DL is a smaller layer inside ML.
  Everything DL is ML, everything ML
  is AI — but not the other way
  around.

  Term:              AI
